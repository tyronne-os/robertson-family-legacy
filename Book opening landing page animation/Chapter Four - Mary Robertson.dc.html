CHAPTER FOUR
The Journey of Mary Robertson

Welcome to Chapter Four. I am Catherine Jacque-Davis, and it is my absolute honor to invite you to step into the story of my dear mother, Mary Robertson. Come along with me on this journey as we celebrate the life of an iconic mother whose unyielding love guided our family from Rosedale to the heart of New Orleans. This is her story.

Born on March 6, 1919, my mother’s beautiful story began in the country town of Rosedale, Louisiana. It was in Rosedale that she met my father, Walter Emmet Jacque, and they began building a life together, quickly welcoming the first two of her eight children, Nelson and Bertha Lee.

Seeking new opportunities, our family relocated to the vibrant Lower Ninth Ward of New Orleans, settling first on Independence Street. It was there that our family grew even larger and filled with more love with the births of Walter, Mary Ethel, Raymond, and Alfred.

As our bustling family continued to expand, we eventually moved into larger accommodations on the corner of Tupelo and Florida Avenue, where my brother Clarence and I (her baby girl, Catherine) were born to complete the family. Though her marriage eventually came to an end, her fierce, motherly love for her children far outlived it, and she stepped into her role, truly becoming the iconic, beloved centerpiece of our family.

Her Pride & Her Joy

Without a question, my mother’s greatest pride and joy was the deeply personal relationship she cultivated with each and every one of her children. Even in a busy house of eight, she had a magical, intentional way of making time to acknowledge, support, and guide all of us individually as we transitioned from children into adults.

Her immense pride was always deeply rooted in her daughters — Bertha Lee, Mary Ethel, and myself, Catherine. She poured her wisdom into us, shaping the women we would become. And without a doubt, her absolute joy and infectious laughter came from her boys — Nelson, Walter, Raymond, Alfred, and Clarence. They kept her on her toes and kept our home filled with endless energy, light, and smiles.

Her Legacy

My mother’s love was a seed that blossomed into an incredibly vast family tree. From her eight beloved children, her legacy beautifully expanded to include an amazing 28 grandchildren, 78 great-grandchildren, and 51 great-great-grandchildren.

The deep bond she shared with her own siblings was so profound that it was even woven into our names. My sister, her second oldest daughter, Mary Ethel, was named in a beautiful tribute to both my mother, Mary, and her older sister, Ethel — a perfect blend of two incredible women.

To know my mother was to know a woman with a deeply nurturing soul and legendary hands in the kitchen. She found great peace in her gardening, tending to the earth just as beautifully as she tended to her family. During the holiday season, her sweet potato pies were absolutely legendary — a warm, delicious staple that we all eagerly anticipated all year long, and a recipe that tasted like pure love.

One of her most cherished daily rituals was picking up the phone to talk to her beloved sisters. Those daily calls were the heartbeat of her afternoons, filled with laughter as they caught up on their favorite soaps, swapped their best kitchen recipes, and, most importantly, chattered endlessly and proudly about all of their grandbabies.

On behalf of myself and all my siblings, it is my absolute honor to formally reintroduce you to the magnificent Mary Robertson. She was our rock, our comfort, and a legendary matriarch. Thank you so much for coming along with me on this journey through Chapter Four to celebrate her beautiful, unforgettable life.
