CHAPTER EIGHT
The Journey of Lamar Robertson

Welcome to Chapter Eight. I am Betty Butler, and it is my absolute honor to invite you to step into the story of my dear father, Lamar Robertson. Come along with me on this journey as we reflect on the life of a truly remarkable, quiet, and strong family man who built a legacy of love from Rosedale to New Orleans. This is his story.

Born in the quiet country town of Rosedale, Louisiana, my father’s journey began with the deep, grounding roots of our family. He later moved to Pine Grove, Louisiana, with his mother. It was there, amidst the peaceful beauty of Pine Grove, that his life would change forever when he met my mother, Cora. The two fell deeply in love, married, and eventually journeyed together to New Orleans, Louisiana, where they set out to build a beautiful family and a life of their own.

His Pride & His Joy

My parents, Lamar and Cora, were profoundly blessed to build a wonderful family together. To their beautiful union, five children were born, and we were, without a doubt, his absolute pride and joy.

My father was a quiet, strong, and incredibly hardworking man who firmly believed in taking care of his family. For most of his life, he worked two jobs, sacrificing his own rest to make absolutely certain that our family was always provided for and well cared for. He was a devoted husband and a fiercely loving father who gave so much of himself to the people he loved.

One of the things that stood out most about my father was how deeply and beautifully he loved my mother, Cora. Growing up, I watched the way he cherished her; it was abundantly clear to everyone that he would do absolutely anything for her and for all of us. His love wasn’t always spoken in loud words; it was shown through his calloused hands, his endless sacrifices, his unwavering protection, and his complete dedication to our home. He was dependable, steady, and always willing to do whatever was necessary for his family.

His Legacy

The quiet strength and unwavering love my father possessed left a lasting impression on everyone who knew him. Today, his beautiful legacy lives on through his five children, thirteen grandchildren, thirty great-grandchildren, and all the family members who were blessed to know and love him.

But his legacy of love extended far beyond our front door — he was a deeply devoted brother who cherished his siblings. As my cousin Tyronne Jacques beautifully recalls, “One of the main memories I have of my uncle Lamar — or as we all affectionately called him, ‘Uncle Man’ — was how he always made his rounds to check on all of his sisters. Once a month, you could count on this powerful, amazing man arriving with his captivating smile. He would walk in, his voice booming with joy, calling out to my grandmother, ‘OH MARY, WHAT’S COOKING TODAY?’ He had that profoundly warm effect on our entire family, and we all cherished those special days when Uncle Man made his rounds.”

That joyous, familiar scene of a brother faithfully looking after his sisters was just another testament to the kind of man he was, and it is a memory our family holds dear.

On behalf of myself and my siblings, it is my absolute honor to formally reintroduce you to our hero, a devoted husband, and the ultimate family man — our father, Lamar Robertson. He will always be remembered for his strength, his hard work, and, most of all, his tremendous love. Thank you so much for coming along with me on this journey through Chapter Eight to celebrate his unforgettable life.

Family Record Update

A loving addition for our family archives: as we celebrate this beautiful history together today, we also warmly remember Betty’s siblings, Marie and James, who have since joined their loving parents in Glory. Their spirits and memories remain a beautiful, cherished part of the Robertson family legacy.
