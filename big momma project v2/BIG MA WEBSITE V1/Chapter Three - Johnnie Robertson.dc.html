CHAPTER THREE
The Journey of Johnnie Robertson

Welcome to Chapter Three. I am Jackie Duchane, and it is my absolute honor to invite you to step into the story of my dear uncle, Johnnie Robertson. Come along with me on this journey as we celebrate the oldest male in our family, a man whose colossal heart and steadfast presence anchored us all to the country soil. This is his story.

As the eldest boy, Uncle Johnnie took his role to heart. Much like Uncle Man, he hovered over his sisters, fiercely protecting and providing whatever he could. He nourished our family from two distinct places: his physical garden, which was always overflowing with fresh Pine Grove harvest greens, and his spiritual garden of deep, unwavering prayer and devotion. He was, quite simply, the uncle of all uncles! He commanded the room with his big voice, gave the most legendary big hugs, and flashed a brilliant, joyful smile. Even with well over a hundred of Big Ma’s grandkids running wildly around the yard, he miraculously remembered every single person’s name.

Uncle Johnnie was another sibling who chose to stay close to home in Pine Grove. He didn’t remain in the country out of a lack of options, but out of a profound, honorable sense of duty. Wherever Big Ma was, you would also find her oldest boy. He was always on watch, always the very first to tackle a task, and always at her call. He served his mother not out of a sense of burden, but purely as a labor of love.

His Pride & His Joy

Even with a whole, vast world out there to explore, Uncle Johnnie knew exactly where his treasure was. He was beautifully united in marriage to Luella Jackson, and together they built a close, loving life right there at home. To their wonderful union, nine children were born: Allen, James, Jannie, Leroy, Louis, Shirley, Leann, Eloise, and Howard. His immense pride only grew as he and Luella were later blessed with six beautiful grandchildren. His family was his world, and keeping them close was his absolute greatest achievement.

His Legacy

The legacy of Uncle Johnnie is forever intertwined with his boundless love for Big Ma and his deep connection to the earth. He was a true farm boy at heart who cherished being on that little stretch of farmland in Pine Grove, proving to us all that there is truly no place like home. We will forever remember the joy he found in the simple things, especially raising his prize hogs alongside Uncle DJ in the backyard of Aunt Beatrice’s home.

On behalf of the countless nieces, nephews, and grandchildren who were blessed to be wrapped in one of his famous hugs, it is my absolute honor to formally reintroduce you to Big Ma’s oldest boy. Thank you so much for coming along with me on this journey through Chapter Three to celebrate the mighty, loving, and unforgettable life of my uncle, Johnnie Robertson.
